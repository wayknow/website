FAQ

Does this work on any website?

If the data is visible on the page, yes. Login-walled and infinite-scroll pages work in most cases.

Is this allowed?

Use it for personal research and respect each site's terms of service. We don't bypass logins or run unattended bulk scraping.

Where does my data go?

With BYOK, extraction happens between your browser and your AI provider — we're never in the path. With Pro credits, requests go to our backend and are never stored beyond the request.

What if the AI extracts the wrong data?

You review the table before exporting. Click any row to correct it — the fix is instant, no re-prompting.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, one click. No contracts.
